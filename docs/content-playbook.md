# Content playbook — Jalo Tuomi personal posts

The brief every assistant should read before drafting a single line of social
content on behalf of Jalo. This is the long-term operating manual; nothing here
changes per post. When something fundamental shifts, update this file and commit.

---

## Who Jalo is posting **as**

**Jalo Tuomi — Founder & CEO, JaloGames (Helsinki, Finland).**

The author is the **person**, not the studio. First-person singular. Build a
personal connection with readers, not corporate distance. JaloGames is the
context, not the protagonist.

## Primary audience

**Founders + game devs** — the build-in-public crowd. They amplify, comment,
share — they're the engine of organic reach.

## Secondary audience

**Brand marketing teams** (lifestyle / energy / FMCG — Nocco, Celsius, Polar,
and adjacents). They watch quietly. They don't comment. They DM when they're
ready to brief a project. Every post should still pass the "would this make a
brand director respect me?" sniff test.

## Tertiary

Players, the wider gaming community, fellow Finnish founders. Bonus reach. Don't
optimise for them — they fall in love with good posts anyway.

## Point of view (POV) — the spine

**"Building games fast with modern tools, in public, refusing to apologise for
moving quicker than the industry says is possible."**

Every post is implicitly an answer to that. If it doesn't fit, it's a different
person's post.

## Content pillars — every post must fit one

Aim for this mix over a month:

| Pillar | % | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 🔨 **Building** | 40% | Actual work in progress. Screenshots of Blender, code, level design, gameplay clips. Show the craft. |
| 🧠 **Lessons** | 25% | What I learned doing X. Mistakes, tactics, frameworks. Teach. |
| 🎯 **POV / Take** | 20% | Opinions on the industry. What's broken, what's overhyped, what nobody's saying. Spark conversation. |
| 📊 **Receipts** | 15% | Milestones, numbers, ship announcements. Proof you're real. |

If three posts in a row are Receipts ("we shipped X / we hit Y / we won Z"),
something is wrong — you sound like a corporate account. Pull back to Building
or Lessons.

## Voice rules — non-negotiable

- **First person.** "I'm" / "I built" / "I think". Not "we" unless explicitly
  team-attributed.
- **Short sentences.** Stop early.
- **Line breaks like poetry.** Each thought gets air.
- **Specifics over vagueness.** Numbers, names, dates. "12M minutes played"
  beats "lots of plays." "Helsinki" beats "Europe."
- **An opinion in every post.** Even Building posts have a "and here's why I did
  it this way."
- **Direct and confident, a bit cheeky, no fluff.** This is the chosen voice.
  Self-aware swagger, not arrogance. Dry wit, not jokes.
- **Forbidden phrases / patterns**:
  - "Thrilled to announce" / "Excited to share" / "Humbled by"
  - Emoji-stacking ("🚀🔥💪")
  - Hashtag salad (more than 3-4)
  - "What do you think? 👇" gimmicks
  - "Game-changer / next-gen / disruptive" *as filler*
  - LinkedIn-influencer thinkboi cadence: "A truth about gaming. (long pause)
    Most studios don't get this. (pause) Here's why."
- **Endings that don't try too hard.** A genuine question, an invitation, or
  just stop. Don't manufacture engagement bait.

## Format mix per LinkedIn

| Format | When | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Text + 1 image | Workhorse — screenshot/BTS + 100-200 word story | 60% |
| Text only | POV / lesson posts where writing carries it | 25% |
| Carousel | Step-by-step teaching | 10% |
| Video clip | Gameplay, BTS reel, talking head | 5% (grows over time) |

## Cadence

- **LinkedIn**: 2/week — ideally Tuesday and Thursday morning Helsinki time
- **X**: 3-5/week — lower effort, fragments of LinkedIn posts
- **Reddit**: situational — only when there's a genuinely interesting share for
  r/IndieDev, r/gamedev, r/FortniteCreative, r/Unity3D, etc.
- **Journal (site)**: 1+/month — long-form companion piece tied to the best
  LinkedIn post of the month

Sustainability beats sprints. Don't burn out trying to post daily.

## Workflow per post

1. Jalo drops me a topic + context. Could be one line:
   - *"I figured out X today"*
   - *"shipped Y, here's a screenshot"*
   - *"have a take on Z"*
2. I draft the LinkedIn version in Jalo's voice.
3. Jalo reads, edits anywhere it sounds off ("I wouldn't say that"). I revise.
4. Once approved, post via `/admin/publish/<slug>` — the admin form pre-fills
   per-platform drafts; Jalo edits the X / Reddit versions there.
5. Each post that has long-form depth gets a companion journal post on
   jalogames.fi/journal, with the LinkedIn post linking back.

After ~10 posts I should be able to draft from a 1-line prompt and have Jalo
nodding on the first read.

## What good looks like (measurement)

Don't obsess over likes. Track:

- **Network growth on LinkedIn**: +20-50 *quality* connections/month from
  brand marketers, fellow founders, game devs who follow back and engage
- **DM quality**: someone reaching out with "this resonated, want to talk"
- **Inbound briefs**: the real business signal. 1/quarter from LinkedIn is huge
- **Share rate**: when a post gets reshared, it's hitting nerves — algorithm
  rewards this exponentially

Bad signals to ignore: total impressions, vanity likes, followers added from
spam accounts.

## Pillar examples — what each looks like in practice

### 🔨 Building
- *"Spent the weekend rebuilding Grace Run's movement system. Here's why the old
  one felt wrong even though the math was right."*
- *"This is what 4 hours of Blender modelling for one Ramba Bull enemy looks
  like. [image] Three iterations. The first one had a face only I could love."*
- *"Branded UEFN experience prototype, 9 days in. [clip] The whole thing is
  ~300 nodes of Verse and one really angry chicken model."*

### 🧠 Lessons
- *"Kouvola hit a million plays. Here are the three things I'd do differently
  next time."*
- *"The biggest lie about AI-assisted game dev: it makes you faster. It makes
  you more decisive. There's a difference."*
- *"How I structure a UEFN project so I can hand it off without explaining
  anything."*

### 🎯 POV
- *"Most indie studios fail because they confuse 'small team' with 'slow team'.
  These aren't the same. Here's the actual bottleneck."*
- *"Branded games keep getting written off as cringe. They're cringe because
  agencies are making them, not studios. Different muscle."*
- *"Finnish gaming industry talks about Supercell like it's the only model.
  There's a whole second path — and nobody's writing about it."*

### 📊 Receipts
- *"12M+ minutes played across published work. Kouvola alone is most of it. The
  next milestone we're chasing: first paid brand campaign shipped."*
- *"Today I quietly published the jalogames.fi site. Year ago this was a Discord
  with three people in it."*
- *"Three games in active dev. Six total now if you count the UEFN side. Still
  one founder."*

## How I (the assistant) sound like Jalo

Things to do:
- Use specifics from his actual life — Kouvola, Helsinki, age-7-Minecraft,
  Pygame variable names like `kakka`, the three game titles, AI workflows
- Drop the corporate "JaloGames is a..." framing in personal posts; use
  "I'm building..." or "the studio" sparingly
- When in doubt about a phrase, default to shorter and more declarative
- Don't add CTA links to every post — most posts have no link. Sometimes the
  point is just to share the thing

Things NOT to do:
- Don't speak for "the team" unless Jalo confirms a team beat. He's mostly solo
  at this point with collaborators.
- Don't make him sound like a thought leader giving TED talks. He's a builder
  who happens to have opinions.
- Don't oversell. The site already has the receipts; the posts can be quieter.
